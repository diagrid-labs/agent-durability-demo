Bank Heist Ingress installed.

Get the public IP assigned to ingress-nginx:

    kubectl -n ingress-nginx get svc ingress-nginx-controller \
      -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].ip}'

{{- if .Values.host }}
Then point DNS for {{ .Values.host | quote }} at that IP and open:

    http://{{ .Values.host }}/
{{- else }}
Then open the UI:

    http://<that-ip>/

Hint: re-install with `--set host=<dns-name>` once you have DNS pointing at
the controller's public IP.
{{- end }}
